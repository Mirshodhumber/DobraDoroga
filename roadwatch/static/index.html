Which road?

Follows one company up the stack: every federal contract over $10,000 (with amendments, sole-source reasons and bid counts), Registry of Lobbyists communications and the PSPC ineligibility list — plus, for a province, its Public Accounts payments and its largest city's awards.     SOURCE MATERIAL

    Add evidence

    Fetches PDFs and web pages directly from government and audit publishers (toronto.ca, ontario.ca, montreal.ca, canada.ca, auditors general and others). The link is saved with the document so every citation traces back to the original.

    Where to find Toronto documents
    • Toronto Bids Portal — awarded contracts since Oct 2025
    • Council & committee documents (TMMIS) — search "award" + a street or contract number for older Bid Award Panel reports
    • Auditor General reports
    • Toronto Open Data

    PDF with selectable text, HTML, TXT, CSV or Markdown. Up to 15 MB and 150 pages. Scanned PDFs need OCR first.

    HOW ROADWATCH WORKS
    The AI & your data
    
    
    Financial checks run on this server with plain arithmetic: they compare award, amendment and payment entries of the same scope and tax basis and flag payments over authorization or repeated references.
    
    
    AI investigation sends the case's documents, entries and observations to the configured model provider (—). The model reads pages, follows leads and returns findings that must quote the source; quotes that don't exist are discarded automatically.
    
    
    Search by meaning sends the text of every evidence page, once, and each search you type to the embedding model (—) so pages can be found by what they say rather than by exact words.
    
    
    Findings are leads for human review. Roadwatch never declares corruption, fraud or misconduct — it points to discrepancies, states innocent explanations, and lists the records needed to resolve them.
    
    
    
    
    Don't upload confidential or whistleblower material unless the server operator has confirmed the AI provider's data terms. Public procurement records are appropriate.
